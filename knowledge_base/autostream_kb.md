# AutoStream Knowledge Base

## About AutoStream
AutoStream is a SaaS platform that provides automated video editing tools for content creators. It uses AI to help creators edit, caption, and publish videos faster.

---

## Pricing Plans

### Basic Plan
- **Price:** $29/month
- **Videos:** 10 videos/month
- **Resolution:** Up to 720p
- **AI Captions:** Not included
- **Support:** Standard support (email, business hours)

### Pro Plan
- **Price:** $79/month
- **Videos:** Unlimited videos/month
- **Resolution:** Up to 4K
- **AI Captions:** Included
- **Support:** 24/7 priority support

## Company Policies

### Refund Policy
- Refunds are available within the first **7 days** of purchase.
- No refunds will be issued after 7 days from the purchase date.

### Support Policy
- **24/7 support** is available exclusively on the **Pro plan**.
- Basic plan users receive standard email support during business hours.

### Billing
- All plans are billed monthly.
- You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time.
- Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I upgrade from Basic to Pro?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade at any time. The upgrade takes effect immediately and you'll be charged the prorated difference.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans, which functions as a risk-free trial.

**Q: What platforms does AutoStream support?**
A: AutoStream works for content creators on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X, and more.

**Q: What video formats are supported?**
A: AutoStream supports MP4, MOV, AVI, and MKV formats for input and exports in MP4.
